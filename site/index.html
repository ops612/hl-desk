HL Desk

Your copied text, screenshots and notes in one small app that lives in the menu bar or taskbar.

ClipboardEverything you copy, searchable. Deletes itself after 1 day; you choose the time.
ScreenshotCapture an area, add numbers or a pen, crop, then drag it into a chat.
NotesQuick notes that save as you type.

Install on Mac

  1. Open the downloaded file and drag HL Desk into Applications.
  2. Open HL Desk. Its icon appears in the menu bar, top right.
  3. Press ⌘ Shift Space to open it, ⌘ Shift 2 to capture an area.

First screenshot: allow HL Desk in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen Recording, then reopen HL Desk.

Install on Windows

  1. Open the downloaded file. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info → Run anyway.
  2. HL Desk starts in the taskbar corner, bottom right.
  3. Press Ctrl Shift Space to open it, Ctrl Shift 2 to capture an area.
Everything stays on your computer. Nothing is uploaded.
